Components of Multimedia

Exploring the major components used in multimedia presentations

Audio

Audio includes music, speech, sound effects, and audio elements used in multimedia.

Images

Images include photographs, drawings, illustrations, icons, and visual graphics.

Video

Video combines moving images and sound to communicate information effectively.

Animation

Animation creates the illusion of movement using sequential graphics and visual effects.

Text

Text provides titles, descriptions, instructions, captions, and written content.
