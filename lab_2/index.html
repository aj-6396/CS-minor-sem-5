Components of Multimedia

Exploring the major components used in multimedia

🎵

Audio

Audio includes music, speech, sound effects, and other sound elements used in multimedia presentations.

🖼️

Images

Images include photographs, drawings, illustrations, icons, and other visual elements.

🎬

Video

Video combines moving images and sound to communicate information in an engaging way.

✨

Animation

Animation creates the illusion of movement using graphics, illustrations, and visual effects.

📝

Text

Text provides titles, descriptions, instructions, captions, and other written information.
